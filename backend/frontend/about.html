About Voxa

Voxa is a web based speech to text tool that helps you convert audio into clean, readable text, then improve it using smart formatting and translation.

The goal is simple: make it easy for anyone to turn speech into usable documents without stress.

What Voxa helps you do

  • Transcribe speech into text using your browser microphone
  • Format transcripts into clean paragraphs and readable structure
  • Translate text into other languages
  • Export results as TXT, DOCX, or PDF

Who it is for

  • Students working on assignments and reports
  • Creators turning voice ideas into scripts
  • Teams writing meeting notes
  • Anyone who prefers speaking over typing
